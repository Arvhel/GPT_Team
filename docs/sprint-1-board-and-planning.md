# Sprint 1 — Board Management and Planning

## Objective

Organize the Sprint 1 board and establish a clear structure for tracking the development tasks of the HOPE, Inc. ERP System.

## Scrum Master Responsibilities

- Organize and manage Sprint 1 GitHub Issues.
- Break project requirements into manageable development tasks.
- Assign responsibilities to the appropriate team members.
- Include acceptance criteria to clarify expected results.
- Monitor task progress through the Scrum Board.
- Coordinate the sprint schedule and project milestones.

## Sprint Planning

Sprint planning focused on identifying the team's responsibilities, organizing development tasks, and establishing a workflow for completing assigned work.

The Sprint 1 board was used to monitor task statuses and provide visibility into the team's progress.

## Task Tracking

GitHub Issues and the Scrum Board served as the primary tools for:

- Recording assigned tasks.
- Identifying responsible team members.
- Monitoring task status.
- Tracking completed work.
- Identifying tasks that required follow-up.

## Expected Output

An organized Sprint 1 board with assigned tasks, clear acceptance criteria, and a structured way to monitor team progress.

## Documentation Note

This document was prepared retrospectively to record the Sprint 1 planning and board management activities.
