\# Hope, Inc. – Sprint 1 Retrospective Follow-up



\## 1. Objective



Review the action items identified during the Sprint 1 retrospective and monitor the team's progress in addressing them during Sprint 2.



\## 2. Identified Sprint 1 Issues



The following issues were identified during the Sprint 1 retrospective:



1\. Late submission of assigned tasks.

2\. Difficulties with the Pull Request process.

3\. Tasks not completed within the planned schedule.



\## 3. Action Items and Progress



| Retrospective Issue | Planned Action | Sprint 2 Progress | Status |

|---|---|---|---|

| Late submission of tasks | Regularly follow up with members and monitor deadlines. | Continued monitoring is needed. | Ongoing |

| Difficulty with Pull Requests | Provide guidance on branches, commits, and Pull Requests. | Members have improved but still experience some difficulties. | Partially Improved |

| Unfinished tasks | Monitor task progress and identify blockers early. | Continued progress monitoring is needed. | Ongoing |



\## 4. Scrum Master Follow-up Activities



\- Monitor assigned task deadlines.

\- Follow up with members regarding pending tasks.

\- Assist members with the Pull Request workflow.

\- Identify and document unresolved blockers.

\- Recommend improvements to the team's workflow.



\## 5. Recommendations



\- Encourage members to provide regular progress updates.

\- Create Pull Requests once assigned work is ready for review.

\- Report blockers as early as possible.

\- Review pending tasks during team check-ins.

\- Continue improving communication and coordination.



\## 6. Conclusion



The team has shown partial improvement in the Pull Request process during Sprint 2. However, late task submissions and unfinished tasks still require monitoring. Continued coordination and follow-ups will help improve the team's workflow in the next sprint.
